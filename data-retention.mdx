## Retention policy

| Table | Retention |
| :--- | :--- |
| `markets` | **90 rolling days** - markets whose `close_time` falls inside the archive window are retained. Older rows are pruned. |
| `snapshots` | **90 rolling days** - snapshots are pruned alongside markets older than 90 days. |

The snapshot worker runs at ~100 ms cadence from open through close, so
a typical 15-minute market produces ~9,000 snapshots. Plan your page
sizes accordingly - the API caps `limit` at 1,000 snapshots per call.

<Note>
Retention is a full rolling 90-day archive. Your subscription still controls
whether your API key can read 31, 60, or 90 days of that archive.
</Note>

<Info>
Plan access is separate from retention. Free accounts can access the newest 50
markets in each `(coin × type)` deployment, subject to a 31-day cap. Shared
paid configurations can access all markets inside their purchased 31-, 60-,
or 90-day window.

An omitted `start_time` is clamped to the cutoff. An explicit timestamp,
cursor, market `close_time`, or snapshot-at timestamp before the cutoff returns
`HISTORY_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`.
</Info>

## Settlement + backfill

Two workers keep the data clean long after a market closes:

- **resolution-worker** stamps `expiration_value`, `result`, and
  `settlement_ts` on the market row once Kalshi publishes the
  settlement.
- **price-backfill** walks unresolved `snapshots` rows and fills
  `coin_price` so replays can line up orderbook state against the
  underlying tick.

Both run continuously, so a market queried 30 seconds after close might
still report `result = null`. Poll again - it usually settles within a
minute.
