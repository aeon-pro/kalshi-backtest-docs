## Retention policy

| Table | Retention |
| :--- | :--- |
| `markets` | Indefinite — every resolved contract stays queryable. |
| `snapshots` | Indefinite — free plan has no history cap. |

The snapshot worker runs at ~100 ms cadence from open through close, so
a typical 15-minute market produces ~9,000 snapshots. Plan your page
sizes accordingly — the API caps `limit` at 1,000 snapshots per call.

## Settlement + backfill

Two workers keep the data clean long after a market closes:

- **resolution-worker** stamps `expiration_value`, `result`, and
  `settlement_ts` on the market row once Kalshi publishes the
  settlement.
- **price-backfill** walks unresolved `snapshots` rows and fills
  `coin_price` so replays can line up orderbook state against the
  underlying tick.

Both run continuously, so a market queried 30 seconds after close might
still report `result = null`. Poll again — it usually settles within a
minute.
