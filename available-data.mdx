## Kalshi up/down crypto markets

Each supported (coin × type) pair is a dedicated Kalshi Workers stack
streaming orderbook snapshots ~100 ms apart from market open through
settlement. Today we ship five deployments, all at 15-minute cadence:

| Coin | v1 segment | Type | Series ticker |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Bitcoin**   | `btc`  | `15m` | `KXBTC15M`  |
| **Ethereum**  | `eth`  | `15m` | `KXETH15M`  |
| **Solana**    | `sol`  | `15m` | `KXSOL15M`  |
| **Dogecoin**  | `doge` | `15m` | `KXDOGE15M` |
| **Ripple**    | `xrp`  | `15m` | `KXXRP15M`  |

<Tip>
v1 URLs are `GET /v1/{coin}/{type}/...` with lowercase segments -
e.g. `/v1/btc/15m/markets`, `/v1/xrp/15m/markets/.../snapshots`.
</Tip>

## What a snapshot contains

Every row in `snapshots.data[]` carries:

- `time` - wall-clock timestamp of the capture (UTC).
- `market_ticker` - the Kalshi market the snapshot belongs to.
- `yes_best_bid`, `no_best_bid` - top-of-book prices on the 0.00-1.00 scale.
- `coin_price` - the underlying coin price at capture time.
- `orderbook_yes`, `orderbook_no` - full top-15 ladders, as
  `{"bids": [[price, qty], ...]}`. Only sent when
  `?include_orderbook=true`.
- `seq` - Kalshi's orderbook-delta sequence number for reconstructing
  diffs relative to the previous snapshot.

## Market metadata

A market row summarises everything static + resolved about a contract:

- `market_ticker` (primary key), `event_ticker`, `series_ticker`
- `title`, `subtitle`, `rules_primary`
- `strike_type`, `floor_strike`
- `open_time`, `close_time`, `expiration_time`
- `coin_price_start`, `coin_price_end` - snapshots at open + close
- `expiration_value`, `result`, `settlement_ts` - the resolved outcome

## Data retention

Snapshots and markets stay queryable for the full 31-day rolling window.
Free keys can access the newest 50 markets per deployment; Pro keys can
access the full 31-day retained window. See [Data retention](/data-retention)
for the full lifecycle.
